# CodeStreet: Monetization Strategy

This document outlines the four core revenue models integrated into CodeStreet to ensure sustainable growth and high profitability for the platform operator. CodeStreet is not just a roadmap tool; it is a financial exchange for software features, and its monetization reflects that.

## 1. The Vig (Transaction Fees)

The platform acts as the clearinghouse for all feature investments. Just like a real stock exchange or a casino, CodeStreet takes a cut of every transaction.

*   **Standard Market Fee (5%):** For every standard pledge ("buy order") placed on a feature, the platform automatically deducts a 5% "Underwriting Fee". This ensures that as the volume of feature trading increases, platform revenue scales linearly.
*   **Dark Pool Fee (15%):** Investors who wish to pledge large amounts anonymously can use the "Dark Pool". Because this service hides their identity and avoids triggering massive "Whale Alerts" immediately, the platform charges a premium 15% fee for this privilege.
*   **Options Contracts Fee (5%):** When users buy speculative Call or Put options on features, a 5% premium fee is collected.

*This revenue is automatically tracked and projected as ARR in the Admin Wealth Dashboard.*

## 2. IPOs (Initial Proposal Offerings)

Getting a feature listed on the CodeStreet Exchange is an event. To prevent spam and ensure only high-quality, serious proposals are added to the backlog, we charge an IPO Listing Fee.

*   **Listing Fee ($50.00):** When a user or a product team "Files an S-1" to list a new feature, a flat listing fee is required. This not only generates immediate revenue but also serves as an effective quality filter.

## 3. PRO Plans (Investor Tools)

CodeStreet is designed to be highly gamified. Competitive investors want an edge to spot undervalued features before they take off.

*   **CodeStreet PRO ($99/month):** A monthly subscription tier targeted at power users and "Market Makers".
    *   **Sniper Mode:** Access to AI-driven analytics that identify undervalued features ("gems") based on commit sentiment, development velocity, and user demand.
    *   **Advanced Trading:** Unlocks the ability to trade Options Contracts (Calls/Puts) and engage in Short Selling.
    *   **Early Access:** View new IPOs 24 hours before the general public.

## 4. SaaS Multitenancy Subscriptions (B2B Expansion - Phase III)

The ultimate goal of CodeStreet is to become the standard infrastructure for public and private company roadmaps.

*   **Workspace Licensing:** Instead of just running one instance of CodeStreet, the platform will support multitenancy. Companies can rent their own isolated CodeStreet instances to manage their specific product roadmaps.
*   **Pricing Tiers:**
    *   **Startup Tier:** Free up to 10 active features, standard 5% transaction fee applies.
    *   **Enterprise Tier ($499/month):** Unlimited features, custom branding (White-label PWA), zero transaction fees (or custom Vig routing back to the company), and advanced API access for Jira/GitHub integration.

---

*By combining micro-transaction fees (The Vig) with premium subscriptions (PRO) and B2B SaaS licensing, CodeStreet is built to be a highly profitable engine that turns software backlogs into capital.*
